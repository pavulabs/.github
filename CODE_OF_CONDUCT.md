# Code of Conduct

**English** | [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Our Pledge

PavuLabs is committed to providing an open, welcoming, safe, and harassment-free collaborative environment for everyone, regardless of age, body characteristics, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## Expected Behavior

- Respect differing viewpoints and discuss issues using facts and technical evidence.
- Give and accept specific, constructive feedback.
- Exercise care regarding security, privacy, and user impact.
- Acknowledge mistakes and correct them proactively.
- Put the interests of the community and users first.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, threats, discrimination, disclosure of another person’s private information without permission, and public disclosure of exploit details for an unpatched vulnerability.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this code and may edit, remove, or reject issues, comments, commits, or other contributions that violate it. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from the project.

To confidentially report a conduct issue, contact the maintainers through the repository’s private GitHub Security Advisory channel and include “Code of Conduct” in the title. Maintainers will make reasonable efforts to protect the reporter’s privacy and handle reports consistently and fairly.

## Scope

This code applies within project spaces and whenever a participant publicly represents Pavu or PavuLabs.

This code is informed by the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
